This one overlaps the Deep work entry beside it, on purpose: I was pairing for
the second half of it and both timers were running. Nothing in Rhizolog objects,
because attention is not exclusive and a tracker that insisted otherwise would
be asking you to lie to it.

An hour that appears in two entries is counted in both. The totals are of
recorded time, not of wall clock, and pretending otherwise would mean deciding
which of the two was the real one.
