# Notes from a talk

Typed during a talk and never filed properly, which is why this is in `scratch/`
rather than in `notes/`. It is about the same thing as [[notes/rust/async]]
either way.

That is the whole demonstration, and it takes two pages in two places to make
it. Ask for everything **under** `notes/rust` and this page is absent — it is in
a different branch, and the hierarchy is telling the truth. Ask for every page
in a **`rust` directory** and it is present, because this wiki has two of those
and the flat reading does not care which one you had in mind.

Both answers are right. They answer different questions, which is why the
dashboard offers both rather than picking: the breadcrumb walks the tree, and
the badge beside the tags ignores it.

[[notes/rhizome]] is the argument for why the second one is not a hack. A tree
says `notes/rust` and `scratch/rust` are unrelated places that happen to share a
name. Anyone who has kept notes for a year knows better.
