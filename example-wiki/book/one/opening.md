Opening
=======

The bus went from a stop with no shelter and no timetable on it, which I took at
the time for a bad sign. I had eleven pounds, a rucksack packed for the wrong
season, and an address copied onto the back of a receipt. Nobody had asked me to
go.

That is the piece I find hardest to explain now, and the piece everybody wants
explained first: no crisis, no letter, nothing anybody could point at afterwards
and call a reason. I woke up on a Tuesday having apparently decided, and spent
the remainder of that week catching up with myself.
