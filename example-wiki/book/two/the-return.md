# The Return

Going back took two days longer than coming out, for no reason I can name beyond
having stopped being in a hurry. The kiosk was where I had left it. So was the
card, so was the weather, and so was the general refusal to commit to anything
before the tide had been consulted.

It was Maren again, with a new tin he wanted looked at before the boat was.

I have spelled his name two ways in this account and I am no longer sure which
of them is right. A woman selling eggs at the top of the pier said Maren and
seemed certain; her son said otherwise — and seemed no less certain. By then it
had begun to feel a rude thing to press, so I wrote both down and left it.
