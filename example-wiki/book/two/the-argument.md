# The Argument at the Lock

We had been walking since the middle of the morning and neither of us had said
anything worth the effort of saying it, which is a state that holds until it
does not.

It came apart at a lock gate, over nothing, in the way these things do, and by
the time we had got the boat through we had both decided separately to pretend
otherwise. It stayed pretended for the rest of the week.

I have taken this out because it arrives too early and answers a question the
account is better for leaving open. It is still where it was.
