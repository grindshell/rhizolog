# Appendix: the timetable

The winter service as it stood that year. It is reproduced here because both
halves of this account lean on it and neither one explains it, and because a
timetable nobody kept to is still a document.

| Day | Out | Back |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | 07:40 | 16:10 |
| Wednesday | 08:05 | 15:45 |
| Friday | 07:40 | 18:25 |
| Saturday | 09:20 | 17:00 |

Sunday there was nothing. Tuesday and Thursday depended on the boat, which is a
sentence the operator would dispute and everybody on the pier would not.
