# Rhizome

A rhizome is a root system with no trunk. Any point connects to any other, there
is no privileged centre, and cutting it in half gives you two of them rather than
two halves.

[[notes/deleuze]] is where the term comes from. The reason it is worth borrowing
for a wiki is the claim underneath it: that knowledge does not actually arrange
itself into a tree, and that filing it as though it did loses the connections
that made it worth keeping.

Which is the whole design here. Pages have slugs that look like paths, but the
paths are for your convenience — the structure that matters is the link graph,
and it is allowed to be a mess.
